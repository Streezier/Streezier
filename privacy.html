Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Streezier collects when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Streezier is a luxury brand. Our slogan is "We trust quality nothing more." If you have any question about this policy, write to us at support.streezier@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your username, email address, phone number, delivery address and password when you create an account. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we cannot see it.
  • Order details: the items you order, your name, delivery address, phone number, email, any coupon used, the amounts, and the date of your order.
  • Cart: the items in your cart are saved in your own browser on your device.
  • Technical information: when your device loads our website or connects to the services we use (such as Google Fonts and Google Firebase), basic technical information like your IP address and browser type is received by those services.
  • Messages: any emails you send us for support.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in.
  • To process, ship and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order or support questions.
  • To keep records and to prevent fraud and misuse of our website.
  • To improve our website and our products.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the store:

  • Google Firebase: stores your account and order information and handles login.
  • Web3Forms: an email service we use to send your order details to us.
  • Delivery partners: your name, delivery address and phone number, so your order can be delivered.
  • Payment providers: when online payment is available, payments will be handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not store your full card details.
  • Authorities: if we are required to do so by law.

5. Storage in your browser

We save your cart in your browser's local storage, and Firebase keeps you logged in using your browser's storage. Logging out or clearing your browser data removes this information from your device.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed for orders, accounting and legal reasons.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including secure online services and access rules so customers can see only their own account and orders. No website can guarantee complete security, so please keep your password private.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support.streezier@gmail.com. We may need to keep some order records where required for legal or accounting reasons.

9. Children

Our website is not meant for children under 18. Please do not create an account if you are under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: support.streezier@gmail.com
